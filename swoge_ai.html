What the colony needs 0

What is real: the tokens, the pools, the prices, the contract safety checks, the holder concentration and the age all come from GeckoTerminal, GoPlus, DexScreener and the blocks of chain 4663 themselves. What is not: no transaction is signed. A position opens at the real price of the moment and closes at the real price of a later reading — that is paper, not execution, and a real buy would also pay gas and slippage. It runs on the server, continuously: the colony does not stop when you close the tab, and everyone sees the same state. This page computes nothing: it asks, and it shows. The agents change their own structure: they reorder their checks on the rejection rates they measured, and an agent whose bucket predicts nothing spawns a specialist that cross-checks it — removed if it does no better. What they cannot change: the safety checks and the stake limits, which live in the code. A system that can learn to lower its own guard always learns it, exactly once. When a reading fails, it is written — and nothing is invented to fill the gap. NFA.
