## Le texte a coller

> **What $SWOBET is for**
>
> $SWOBET is not a chart play. Buying it to trade it misses the point — and
> we would rather say so than let anyone find out the hard way.
>
> It has one job: to be the token the sportsbook runs on.
>
> Here is how it is meant to work. You deposit $SWOGE. You place your bet in
> $SWOGE. Behind the scenes the bet is carried in $SWOBET, and every
> conversion is done for you — you never touch it, never think about it, and
> never need to hold it to play. The book keeps its own books.
>
> Why bother? Because a sportsbook that settles in $SWOGE puts every stake
> and every payout straight onto $SWOGE. A handful of bettors, nobody
> notices. A few thousand, and the betting flow becomes the price. Giving the
> book its own token keeps that flow where it belongs — in the book — instead
> of on the $SWOGE chart.
>
> So nobody has to be talked into buying $SWOBET. If the sportsbook gets
> used, demand comes from the betting, not from a pitch.
>
> **Status: not live yet.** Bets today are settled in $SWOGE. $SWOBET is
> deployed and tradeable, but the sportsbook does not use it. We will say so
> here the day it does.
