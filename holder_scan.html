How believer and serpent are calculated

Believer — a wallet that still holds tokens and has held for more than 2 days. It stuck around through time, even if it sold a little along the way. Conviction is measured by staying, not by never touching the sell button.

Serpent — a wallet that sold and no longer holds, or one that sold while arriving less than 2 days ago. A quick in-and-out, not a believer.

Neutral — holds a bag but arrived too recently to count as a believer yet, or moved tokens between wallets without selling to the pool.

This is an automatic on-chain read: it sees flows, not intentions. A “serpent” may have simply rebalanced, a “believer” may just be inactive. Read it as a signal, not a verdict.
