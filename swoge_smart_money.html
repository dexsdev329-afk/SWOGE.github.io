Smart Money

Robinhood Chain tokens that went over $1M, and their first 50 buyers, read on chain: when each wallet entered, the ETH it paid and got back (fees included), what it still holds, and whether it came out ahead. Wallets that buy in the same block as others, relay tokens or get WETH from the creator carry an insider pattern mark and are never called smart. Funding in native ETH leaves no log: it is not read.
