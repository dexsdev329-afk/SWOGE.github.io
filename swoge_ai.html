What is realThe tokens, the pools, the prices, the contract safety checks, the holder concentration and the age all come from GeckoTerminal, GoPlus, DexScreener and the blocks of chain 4663. Mirror results are measured on the wallets themselves.
What is paperNo transaction is signed by the colony: a paper position opens and closes at real quoted prices, and a real buy would also pay gas and slippage. The mirror results show how much that costs. Only pairs quoted in ETH are traded.
Where decisions are madeThe colony runs on the server, continuously: it does not stop when you close the tab, and everyone sees the same state. This page computes nothing — it asks, and it shows. Safety checks and stake limits live in the code; when a reading fails, it is written, and nothing is invented to fill the gap. NFA.
