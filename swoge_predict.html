PancakeSwap Prediction · the real game (paper)

This is the one that matters. It reads the real PancakeSwap Prediction rounds on BNB Chain — the live price, the two pools and their payout odds — and guesses BULL/BEAR. It only would bet when the odds make it positive-EV; when the payout is poor it skips. A martingale sizes the stake so a win can claw back the losing streak. Paper only, nothing signs — this measures whether the strategy survives before a real wallet plays it (stage 2).
