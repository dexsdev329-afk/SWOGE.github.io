Solana & Ethereum colonies

Before a colony trades a new chain, it watches it. Every new token on Solana and Ethereum is read at its first price and again 30 minutes later, with its traits: pool size, market cap, venue, security, holders. A paper bank then buys some of them at the venue’s real quotes, fees included — no real money until a case is measured to pay, with the number of buys it rests on.

Does anything stand out?

A case stands out only if its average 30-minute move is above zero beyond chance: its t must clear a bar that rises with the number of cases tested (many tries make lucky ones). Each move is capped at +300% first — a token first read at a near-zero price would otherwise count for billions of %. This is before fees: a case that stands out is a lead for step 2, not a trade.

The paper bank Paper only · real quotes

Step 2 has started. Each buy is priced like a real order: a buy quote and a sell-back quote for the tokens received — no sell route, no buy. The same buy is then priced for sale at 10, 30 and 60 minutes; the bank settles at 30, net of price impact, DEX fees and network costs. Nothing is signed, no key exists, and the real-money mirror never follows this bank.

Control and arms

The control buys the first token after every 15 minutes without looking at it. Each arm buys only the tokens of one case standing out above — or, on Robinhood, one group the SWOGE AI colony handles: its own buys (“colony · bought”, the colony’s control) and four groups it refuses. Each token counts once, even if bought again. An arm losing beyond chance is retired; one only “holds in paper” with 100+ tokens, t above the bar and both halves positive.

Who pushes their tokens?

The dev is the wallet that sent the token’s first mint. For each of their launches with a plausible launch cap ($1k–$2M — tokens born at absurd caps are left out), we compare the highest cap seen at 30 min, 2 h, 6 h and 24 h with the cap at first price. A token that vanished counts as zero. This is a lower bound of the real peak, and a dev becomes a buying rule only once “dev history” is measured as a trait.
