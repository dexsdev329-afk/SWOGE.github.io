— is the coin that pays gas here. It is ether, and the chain's own symbol for it really is ETH — but it is not the same balance as ether on Ethereum mainnet: a different network holds different funds. This wallet writes the chain into the name so the two are never taken for one.

Elsewhere it will still read ETH: your browser wallet, the block explorer and the add-network prompt all use the chain's own symbol, and this page cannot rename what they show. Same coin, shorter name.

This wallet is Robinhood Chain only, because that is where $SWOGE and $SWOGEBET live. No other network is offered — showing one you cannot use would only invite a loss.

MY ACCOUNTS

Every account here comes from the same recovery as the first one — they are derived from it, the way a browser wallet derives its accounts. One recovery brings them all back.

YOUR KEY

Your key gives full control of this address, for ever. Anyone who sees it owns it. SWOGE never sees it and can never recover it for you — Privy shows it to you directly, in its own window. Write it down somewhere only you can reach, and never paste it into a message, a form, or a support chat.
