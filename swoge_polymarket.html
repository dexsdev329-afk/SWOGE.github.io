How to read a trading screenshot

  1. Count the bets. 18 wins out of 27 sounds strong, but a coin toss does that about 6% of the time. Under a hundred resolved bets, luck and skill look the same.
  2. Count the traders. A board of 17 bots will almost always show one on a hot streak. Showing the best one, at its best hour, proves nothing.
  3. A win rate means nothing without the price paid. Buying at 80¢ and winning 67% of the time loses money. The skill score above compares what won with what was paid.
  4. Look at the worst stretch, not the best hour. Bots that provide liquidity get hit when the price jumps; losses come in bursts.
  5. Ask for the wallet. Every Polymarket trade is public. A trader who has an edge can show it here.
