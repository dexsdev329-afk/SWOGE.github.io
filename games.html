✉️ Sign in with an email

The fastest way in — no app, no extension, nothing to install.

  1. Open any game and press Sign in with email.
  2. Type your address. We send you a 6-digit code.
  3. Enter the code. A wallet is created for you automatically.

That account is the same everywhere: Spin, Poker, Blackjack, the casino tables, the Pusher, Smash and the staking page all share one balance.

👛 Connect your own wallet

Already have a wallet? Press Connect wallet and pick it.

  1. Approve the connection in your wallet.
  2. Sign the message we show you. It only proves the address is yours — it is not a transaction and costs no gas.
  3. If you are on another network, accept the switch to Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663).

Your keys never leave your wallet. We only ever see your public address and signed transactions.

⬇️ Deposit $SWOGE

Depositing moves $SWOGE from your wallet into your in-game balance. It happens on the staking page, in the Deposit $SWOGE panel.

  1. Enter an amount, or tap 10% / 20% / 50% / MAX.
  2. Approve once — this lets the vault contract move your $SWOGE. You only do it the first time.
  3. Press Deposit and confirm the transaction.

Once the transaction is mined the amount shows up in your balance and works in every game. You need a little ETH on Robinhood Chain to pay the gas.

🔒 How staking works

Staking locks part of your balance and pays you yield for holding it there.

  1. Go to the staking page.
  2. Choose an amount — or tap 10% / 20% / 50% / MAX.
  3. Press STAKE NOW.

From that moment the yield accrues every second. CLAIM YIELD collects what has accrued so far and puts it back in your balance; UNSTAKE ALL returns your stake. While it is staked, that $SWOGE is out of circulation.

🔥 Burning $SWOGE

Burning sends tokens to 0x…dEaD, an address nobody holds the keys to. They can never be moved again, so they leave the circulating supply for good.

  1. Open the burn page and connect your wallet.
  2. Pick how much — 10%, 25%, 50% or 100% of your balance.
  3. Press Burn forever and confirm.

The page shows the running total burned and what share of the supply that represents. It is permanent and irreversible — there is no undo.

🎮 The games and their rules

SWOGE Spin — a 3×3 slot. Line up $SWOGE symbols; three volcanoes trigger the Hold & Push bonus and its coin jackpots.

SWOGE Poker — Texas Hold’em, 6 seats, real players only, never a bot. One minute per decision. The house takes 5% of the pot, and only when a flop is dealt.

SWOGE Blackjack — blackjack pays 3:2, the dealer stands on 17.

Casino Hold’em & Three Card — heads-up against the house, instant, no waiting for anyone. Pick a chip, tap a betting spot, tap the red × to take it back. Full paytables are behind the i button on the table.

Mines — a grid of 25 tiles hiding the number of mines you chose. Turn tiles over one at a time; every safe tile pushes the multiplier up, and you cash out whenever you like. Hit a mine and the stake is gone. The mines are placed the moment you deal, never as you click — you can recompute the whole grid once the seed is revealed. The house edge of 3% is taken once, so opening one tile or twenty returns the same 97% on average.

Hi-Lo — one card is face up. Call higher or lower: guess right and the multiplier climbs, guess wrong and the stake is gone. Cash out whenever you like. The same rank is a tie — the step is replayed, no win, no loss. Each announced multiplier already includes the house edge of 3% per step, so the odds never depend on how bold you are.

Plinko — pick a board (8, 12 or 16 rows), a risk level and a stake, then drop the ball. Each row sends it left or right, and the cup it lands in decides the payout: the middle cups pay less than your stake, the outside ones far more — up to 78× on the wildest board. The cup is settled from the seeds the moment you drop, so the animation only retells a result already decided. The house edge of 3% is built into every table.

Coin Pusher — the arcade classic on-chain, one shared table for everybody, with a live jackpot.

SWOGE Smash — one smash, one multiplier, up to 250×.

🎲 Provably fair — what it means

Every round is decided by two seeds: one from the server, one from you.

  1. Before you play, the server publishes the hash of its secret seed. You can see it at the bottom of the game.
  2. Your own seed goes into the mix, so the server cannot know the outcome in advance either.
  3. After the round the server reveals its seed. Hash it yourself: if it matches what was published, nothing was changed mid-hand.

That is the whole point — you do not have to trust us, you can check.
